🔥This project, Brgy. Tanod S.O.S., is designed to bridge the critical communication gap between residents and local authorities during emergencies. It transforms a standard smartphone into a life-saving tactical tool for your community.
Below is a comprehensive breakdown designed for your presentation to the local Barangay council.

🛡️ Brgy. Tanod S.O.S. | Project


🔴 Breakdown


The Vision
To create a "Zero-Delay" safety net for every household. No more busy phone lines or searching for numbers—just instant, high-tech coordination between Citizens, Tanod Patrols, and the Barangay Hall.

🆘1. For the Citizens (The Resident App)
The app provides every resident with a personal "Emergency Button" and a digital guardian.
The SOS Command Center: A massive, high-visibility button to report Fire, Medical, Crime, or Flooding.
The Guardian Button (AI Listener):
What it is: A specialized "Always-On" safety mode.
How it works: When activated, the Guardian AI uses the phone's microphone to listen for specific distress patterns (like screaming, shouting, or loud crashing sounds).
The Benefit: If someone is attacked or falls and cannot reach their phone, the Guardian AI detects the shout and automatically initiates an SOS protocol, alerting the Tanods and starting video recording.

Live Evidence Streaming: Once an SOS is triggered, the app automatically records and streams video back to the command center to ensure evidence is captured instantly.
Real-Time Unit Tracking: Residents can see the live GPS location of the responding Tanod unit on a map, providing peace of mind that help is on the way.
Tactical SOS Chat: A dedicated channel to talk directly to the responding team while they are en route.

🚨2. For the Responders (The Tanod Patrol)
Equips our Tanod officers with modern tactical tools to respond faster and more effectively.
Electronic Patrol Log: Replaces paper logs with live GPS tracking. The Barangay always knows exactly which street is being patrolled.
Instant Dispatch Alerts: When a resident hits the SOS button, every patrolling Tanod receives a high-priority alert with the exact GPS coordinates and nature of the emergency.
Tactical Maps: Integrated navigation that guides the Tanod unit through the quickest route to the victim's location.
Evidence Collection: Responders can view the live report and any uploaded photos/videos before they even arrive, allowing them to prepare for the situation.

🗃️📊3. For the Barangay Hall (The Command Center)
A "God's Eye" view for the Barangay Captain and Administrators to manage the whole community's safety.

Global Tactical Map: A live dashboard showing every active patrol and every reported incident across the entire Barangay.

Guardian Broadcast System: The ability to send instant, Barangay-wide emergency announcements (e.g., Typhoon warnings, Fire alerts, or Security notices) that bypass standard SMS delays.

Resident Verification: A secure system to verify all residents, ensuring only legitimate community members can access the SOS network.

Incident Analytics: Tracks where and when most crimes or accidents happen, allowing the Barangay to adjust patrol routes to high-risk areas.

📲4. Key Technological Advantages

Offline Ready: The app is built for Philippine conditions. If the signal is weak, the SOS request is "queued" and will automatically sync the moment a connection is found.

Low Battery Usage: Designed with a "Tactical Dark Mode" and optimized GPS pings to ensure the app stays running even on older phones.

AI-Enhanced Safety: Uses Google Gemini and specialized AI to analyze incident reports, helping the Barangay prioritize which emergencies need the most urgent attention.
